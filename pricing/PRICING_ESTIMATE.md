# Vasavi Penugonda Revamp — Pricing Estimate

## Recommended commercial position

- **Ask:** ₹10,00,000 + GST, if applicable
- **Target close:** ₹9,00,000–₹9,50,000 + GST
- **Do not go below:** ₹8,00,000 unless the client supplies a working backend/API and removes the custom admin/content system, payment rework, or migration responsibilities.
- **Estimated delivery:** 14–16 calendar weeks full-time; 20–24 weeks if undertaken part-time.

This is a fixed-scope estimate for a new API-driven website and content-management workflow, not a promise to discover and rebuild unknown private backend behavior for free.

## Included baseline

- 17 successful public HTML routes, including the home page, information pages, news, newsletters, Vasavi Dham, story, trustees, galleries, videos, donations, contact, and privacy.
- Migration/cleanup of the currently visible content, up to 10 public documents and approximately 150 referenced assets, subject to the client supplying missing originals where current URLs are broken.
- Responsive visual redesign and reusable design system.
- API/content model and a basic editor/admin workflow for pages, news/events, documents, projects, trustees, gallery albums/media, and donation programs.
- Frontend rendering from API responses rather than hardcoded page content.
- Three donation journeys, contact form, newsletter subscription, YouTube embeds, maps, media/document handling, and payment-provider integration subject to valid client credentials and provider approval.
- Redirect map, metadata, accessibility/performance pass, responsive QA, deployment, documentation, and 30 days of defect warranty.

Suggested payment collection: 20% on kickoff, 15% after approved UX/IA, 25% after backend/API staging, 25% after frontend staging, and 15% at launch/handover.

## Exclusions to state in the proposal

Content rewriting, translation/multilingual support, new photography/video, mobile-app work, CRM/ERP integrations, donor accounting/reconciliation, advanced roles/permissions, bulk content beyond the captured baseline, server-log/database recovery, hosting and third-party fees, payment-provider charges, and ongoing marketing/SEO retainers.

The first milestone should include a client-access checkpoint for the existing database, admin panel, mail delivery, hosting, payment account, and any private API. If those reveal additional systems or workflows, price them through a written change request.
